About / Madrid, Spain

About me.

I’ve spent nine years working across brand, design and creative operations, including five running an independent studio for more than 35 clients.

At VeUP, I joined as the first in-house Creative Lead and progressed into Marketing Growth and Operations Manager, retaining creative ownership as my remit expanded into workflows, company systems and AI-assisted production.

My leadership has centred on distributed freelance specialists and partners: setting the brief, allocating work, giving feedback and maintaining quality. I’m based in Madrid, Spain.
