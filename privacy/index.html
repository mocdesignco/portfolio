Optional analytics

With your permission, I use Microsoft Clarity to understand which pages people visit, where they click and how far they scroll. This helps me improve the portfolio’s content and navigation.

Clarity can collect page URLs, referral information, device and browser details, approximate location and interactions on this website. Its heatmaps aggregate those interactions. Session replays reconstruct browsing activity within this site; they do not record your camera or your screen outside it.

The Clarity script loads only after you select “Accept analytics”. Selecting “Decline” leaves Clarity off. You can use the portfolio either way.

Your choice

Analytics is off until you accept.

Your choice and the date it was made are stored in this browser for up to 180 days. This local preference remembers both acceptance and refusal. Clearing browser storage removes it.

You can reopen these controls through “Privacy & analytics” in the footer of any page. Withdrawing consent stops the tracker, removes Clarity’s first-party cookies where accessible and reloads the page with analytics off. It does not automatically erase information already collected.

Microsoft Clarity and cookies

After you accept, Clarity uses cookies and other technologies to associate visits and produce analytics. Its first-party cookies include _clck and _clsk. This site sends an analytics consent signal and denies advertising storage consent; it does not request permission for advertising tracking.

Interaction data is sent to Microsoft for processing. Microsoft’s handling of data, including international transfers, is described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Read more about Clarity cookies.

Retention and privacy requests

Microsoft currently documents a 30-day retention period for ordinary session playback and nine months for heatmap data and sessions marked as favourites or labelled. See Clarity’s retention information.

For questions or requests about data associated with your visit, contact mocdesignco@gmail.com. You may also contact your local data protection authority; in Spain, this is the AEPD.
