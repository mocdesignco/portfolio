Optional analytics
Microsoft Clarity project z272gi9258 is loaded through shared/analytics.js only after explicit analytics consent.
Acceptance and refusal are remembered for 180 days in moc-portfolio-analytics-v1 localStorage. Advertising storage consent is denied.
The footer opens preferences. Withdrawal stops the tracker, clears accessible first-party Clarity cookies and reloads the page with tracking off.
Privacy details are at privacy/index.html. Do not add the raw tracking snippet separately; that would bypass consent and duplicate tracking.
